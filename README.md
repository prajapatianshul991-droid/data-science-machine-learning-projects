# Retail Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data using Python. The main goal is to understand sales performance, identify popular products, compare revenue across categories and cities, and study the impact of discounts on overall revenue.

## Dataset
- **Rows:** 500
- **Columns:** 10 original columns
- **Data:** Synthetic retail sales dataset

The dataset includes order details, dates, products, categories, prices, quantities, cities, payment methods, customer ratings, and discounts.

## Tools & Libraries
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook

## Work Performed
1. Explored the dataset using Pandas.
2. Checked and handled missing values.
3. Checked for duplicate records.
4. Created a new revenue column using price × quantity.
5. Analyzed revenue by product, category, city, and payment method.
6. Compared average customer ratings across products.
7. Analyzed discounts by category.
8. Created visualizations to understand sales trends.
9. Calculated discount amounts and net revenue.
10. Identified important business insights from the analysis.

## Key Findings
- Electronics generated the highest category revenue of ₹21,04,609.
- Monitor was the highest revenue-generating product, with ₹6,70,748.
- Noida recorded the highest city-wise revenue of ₹3,88,290.
- Cash was the highest revenue-generating payment method.
- Monitor had the highest average customer rating.
- Lifestyle products had the highest average discount.
- Total revenue before discounts was ₹36,35,152.
- Net revenue after discounts was ₹33,58,583.75.
